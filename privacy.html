Last updated: October 30, 2026

Casino King Tycoon (“the game”, “we”) is a casino management game for entertainment only. It does not offer real-money gambling, and in-game coins and gems have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes. This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (your casinos, coins, gems, settings) is saved on your device. It stays there until you uninstall the game or reset your progress in Settings.

2. Optional account and cloud save

If you choose to sign in with Google, we use Google Firebase Authentication to create an account for you. We receive a user ID, your display name and your email address. Your game progress is then also saved in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, linked to that user ID, so you can restore it on another phone. Signing in is optional; the game works without an account.

3. Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show ads, limit fraud and measure ad performance. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We receive a purchase confirmation so we can deliver the item you bought.

5. Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example which Casino Hall level players reach and when they stop playing), so we can improve the game balance. It collects app usage events, device information and an app instance ID. We do not use it to identify you personally.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when a construction is finished). They are created on your device and are not sent through our servers.

7. Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed only by the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play) to run the game.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Account and cloud save data are kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account and cloud save at any time in the game: Settings → Delete my account. You can also ask us to delete it by email (see below); see how to delete your account. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months by Google Firebase.
